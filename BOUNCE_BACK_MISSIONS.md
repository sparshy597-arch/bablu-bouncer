# ⚡ HACKATHON THEME: "BOUNCE BACK"
## SPARSH - 3D Open World Driving Game & Physics Sandbox

> **Theme Interpretation**: In life, racing, and physics, setbacks are inevitable. You will crash, flip upside down, lose control, and face destruction. But true champions **BOUNCE BACK** — stronger, smarter, and faster than before.

---

## 🏆 The 8 "Bounce Back" Missions

| # | Mission | Icon | Goal / Requirement | Thematic Story | In-Game Trigger |
|---|---|:---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Time Rebound** | ⏳ | Rewind time **3 times** using the 5s Time Travel feature | *Made a fatal turn or smashed into a wall? Bounce back 5 seconds in time and rewrite your history!* | Press **`T`** or **`Z`** (or click the `⏳` HUD icon). |
| **2** | **Phoenix Rebirth** | 🔄 | Respawn after crashing or falling off **3 times** | *Falling off the edge into the abyss is not the end. Respawn and bounce right back into the race!* | Press **`R`** or fall off the floating island. |
| **3** | **Spring Loaded** | 🦘 | Bounce vehicle suspensions **15 times** | *Use custom hydraulic springs to literally bounce your car over terrain, bumps, and obstacles!* | Press **`Space`** or `Numpad 1-9`. |
| **4** | **Turtle Reversal** | 🐢 | Flip back upright after landing upside down **2 times** | *Knocked upside down like a helpless turtle? Activate the automatic unstuck spring to bounce back on all 4 wheels!* | Flip car upside down on terrain until unstuck spring fires. |
| **5** | **Acrobatic Recovery** | 🤸 | Perform **2 flips** and stick a clean 4-wheel landing | *Launch off massive ramps and jumps, perform backflips or frontflips, and bounce back into a smooth landing!* | Ramp off terrain and land front or back flips. |
| **6** | **Rise from the Ashes** | 🔥 | Sacrifice your vehicle in the Altar and resurrect | *Drive into the dark sacrificial void of chaos, hear the death toll, and rise from the ashes alive.* | Drive directly into the glowing red beam at the Altar. |
| **7** | **World Restoration** | 🏕️ | Rest at the Bonfire to rebuild the island | *When crates are smashed, pins scattered, and chaos reigns, rest at the eternal flame to restore the world!* | Drive up to the Landing bonfire and press **`E`** (Res(e)t). |
| **8** | **Pinball Ricochet** | 🎳 | Rebound off the Mega Bowling bumpers **5 times** | *Carom your vehicle off the heavy lane bumpers and bounce straight back into the strike zone!* | Toggle bumpers at Bowling alley and ricochet off them. |

---

## 🕹️ Interactive In-Game HUD
- Click the glowing orange-red **`⚡` (Bounce Back)** button on the top-right of your screen.
- A glassmorphism **Hackathon Mission Panel** will appear showing your live real-time progress for all 8 missions, complete with progress bars and completion checkmarks!
- Each mission completed also pops up an in-game achievement banner with sound fanfare!

---

## 🚀 How to Test & Demonstrate for Hackathon Judges

1. **Local Server**: Run `npm run dev` in `folio-2025-main` -> Open `http://localhost:5173/`
2. **Live Public GitHub Link**: Open `https://sparshy597-arch.github.io/sparsh-game/`
3. Click the **`⚡`** icon on the top right to show the judges the live mission tracker.
4. Drive forward, press **`T`** to demonstrate **Time Rebound (5s rewinding)**.
5. Press **`Space`** to show **Spring Loaded (hydraulic bouncing)**.
6. Drive off the edge and press **`R`** to show **Phoenix Rebirth (respawning)**.
